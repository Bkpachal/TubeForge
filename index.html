How Does TubeForge Work?

TubeForge follows a simple three-stage process: import an existing page, choose the visual treatment and export the finished video. Users can repeat the process for additional pages within their monthly render allowance.

1

Step 1: Prepare Your Coloring Page

Choose a coloring-book page that contains clear outlines and enclosed white areas. TubeForge is designed primarily for coloring pages, although the sales page also describes certain uses for word searches and crosswords.

2

Step 2: Upload a PDF or Image

Drop a complete PDF into the app to split it into individual pages, or upload PNG and JPG images. The vendor states that rendering takes place on the user's computer and the artwork is not uploaded to a server.

3

Step 3: Choose a Video Template

Select one of the three included styles: Marker Coloring, Rainbow Coloring or Neon Line-Trace. Two additional formats are advertised as part of the optional Activity Pack upgrade.

4

Step 4: Customize the Video

Choose from four camera movements, select a hook from the 52 options across 13 niches, and pick a music mood or use the included marker ASMR sound. A pattern-interrupt opener can optionally show the finished artwork before the animation begins.

5

Step 5: Render and Export

Render the selected page and download the resulting vertical video. The advertised output is 1080×1920 HD MP4 without a watermark on supported browsers. WebM is offered as a fallback on browsers that cannot encode H.264.

6

Step 6: Publish Your Video Manually

Upload the exported clip to your preferred social platform and add the relevant book link to your profile or post. TubeForge does not automatically post videos or manage social media accounts, so publishing remains the user's responsibility.
